Triggered when a guest asks for their car. Same card/notification pattern as the waiter app. Tab counts update live; vehicle and bay come from the guest/PMS record (guest tablet, valet flow). A ticket already claimed by a colleague still shows here, read-only, once it runs past its SLA — so a free attendant can see it needs help without waiting for the front desk's oversight board to flag it first.
